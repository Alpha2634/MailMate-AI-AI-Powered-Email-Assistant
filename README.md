# 📧 MailMate AI – Your Smart Email Assistant

**MailMate AI** is an AI-powered email assistant designed to make email management easier, faster, and smarter. Built with Python, Hugging Face Transformers, and Gradio, it uses the Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct language model to help users understand and respond to emails efficiently.

Simply paste an email, choose your preferred reply tone, and let MailMate AI do the work!

### ✨ What Can MailMate AI Do?

- 📂 **Email Classification:** Automatically categorizes emails into Work, Personal, Meeting, Complaint, Sales, or Other.
- 🚦 **Priority Detection:** Identifies the importance and urgency of incoming messages.
- 📝 **Smart Summarization:** Converts lengthy emails into short, meaningful summaries.
- ✅ **Action Item Extraction:** Highlights important tasks and follow-ups.
- 💬 **AI Reply Generation:** Creates suggested replies in Professional, Friendly, and Formal tones.
- 🛡️ **Basic Security Checks:** Flags potentially suspicious links, urgent payment requests, and requests for sensitive information.
- 📊 **Structured Results:** Displays email analysis in an organized JSON format.

### 🛠️ Built With

Python | Hugging Face Transformers | Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct | PyTorch | Gradio | Google Colab

### 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of MailMate AI is to explore how Large Language Models (LLMs) and prompt engineering can solve everyday communication challenges through a practical AI application.
